Article 1: Health Information: The health information that were processed and were given a visual, audible or readable indication
Article 13: The health information and data related to the health services provided in the State may not be stored, processed,
Article 13: generated or transferred outside the State, unless in the cases defined by virtue of a decision issued
Article 13: by the Health Authority in coordination with the Ministry.
Article 24: Whoever violates the provisions of Article (13) of this Law shall be punished by a fine of not less than

## Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data
Article 1: Cross-Border Processing: Dissemination, use, display, transmission, reception, retrieval, sharing or processing of Personal Data outside the State.
Article 2: Personal Health Data that has legislation regulating its protection and processing.
Article 2: Governmental entities which control or process Personal Data.
Article 18: The Data Subject shall have the right to object to any decisions resulting from automated processing, including profiling.
Article 22: Personal Data may be transferred to outside of the State in the following cases approved by the Bureau.
Article 23: If the transfer is necessary to protect the public interest.
Article 23: If there is an explicit consent granted by the Data Subject to transfer his/her Personal Data outside the State.
