# Notes on the UAE Personal Data Protection Law

Our own summary of the law as read on the official portal. Read the full text before relying on it.

Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021 excludes government data and personal health data that has its own legislation.

Transfers abroad may rest on an approved adequacy route under Article 22 or the listed cases in Article 23, which include explicit consent and protection of the public interest.

The law refers to a Bureau as the competent body. The Article 23 text extracted here does not say who decides that a crisis transfer serves the public interest.
