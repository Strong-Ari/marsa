# Notes on health-data transfer exceptions

Our own summary of law-firm commentary (CMS, Al Tamimi, Trowers & Hamlins), with sources in the manifest. Read the Ministerial Decision itself before relying on it.

Ministerial Decision 51 of 2021 lists exceptions to the transfer restriction in Article 13 of Federal Law 2 of 2019.

The listed bases include treatment outside the UAE, examination of samples, scientific research, insurance, cooperation with the UAE state, pharmacovigilance, online health services, personal health devices and patient request.

Many exceptions depend on patient consent and encoding of the data, and some require a copy to stay in the UAE.

The exception list as summarised here does not name network transit or crisis rerouting.
