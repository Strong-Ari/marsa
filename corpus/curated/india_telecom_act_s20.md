# India, Telecommunications Act 2023, section 20: short verbatim extracts (retrieved 2026-11-03)
Section 20(1): On the occurrence of any public emergency, including disaster management, or in the interest of public safety
Section 20(1)(a): take temporary possession of any telecommunication service or telecommunication network from an authorised entity
Section 20(1)(b): messages of a user or group of users authorised for response and recovery during public emergency are routed on priority
